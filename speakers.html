Invited expert

Paulina Rajala

Development Manager, InstituteQ
National Quantum Institute of Finland

Paulina Rajala is Development Manager at InstituteQ, the National Quantum Institute of Finland. She leads strategic planning, operational coordination, and community-building to support Finland’s national quantum technology initiative. Her work connects researchers, institutions, and stakeholders nationally and internationally.

Previously, she worked in science and technology diplomacy at the UK Foreign Office. She holds a PhD in Microbiology from the University of Helsinki and has over a decade of research experience, including roles at VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland and Nanyang Technological University.

Invited expert

Prof. Kimmo Halunen

Professor of Cybersecurity
University of Oulu & National Defence University, Finland

Kimmo Halunen received his D.Sc. (Tech.) in computer engineering in 2012, specializing in hash function security. He has authored over 60 publications on security, cryptography, cyberwarfare, and blockchain technology. He has coordinated a national project in post-quantum cryptography and led national and international cybersecurity and cryptography projects.

He has provided expert input to the Finnish government and Parliament. Before becoming a professor, he worked at VTT for eight years and visited KU Leuven’s COSIC group in 2014–2015. Since July 2024, he has also been a board member of Xiphera, a Finnish cryptographic hardware company.
