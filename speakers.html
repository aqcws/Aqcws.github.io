Meet our speakers

Researchers and professionals contributing to the Applied Quantum Computing Winter School. Lecture and panel roles will be updated as the programme is confirmed.

Paulina Rajala

Development Manager · InstituteQ, National Quantum Institute of Finland

Paulina Rajala is Development Manager at InstituteQ, the National Quantum Institute of Finland, where she leads strategic planning, operational coordination, and community-building to support Finland’s national quantum technology initiative. Working at the intersection of strategy, science, and collaboration, she connects researchers, institutions, and stakeholders nationally and internationally.

Rajala previously worked in science and technology diplomacy at the UK Foreign Office, contributing to science and innovation policy. She holds a PhD in Microbiology from the University of Helsinki and has more than a decade of research experience, including positions at VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland and Nanyang Technological University.

Prof. Kimmo Halunen

Professor of Cybersecurity · University of Oulu and National Defence University, Finland

Kimmo Halunen received his D.Sc. (Tech.) in computer engineering in 2012, researching hash function security. He has authored more than 60 publications on cybersecurity, cryptography, cyberwarfare, and blockchain technology. He has coordinated a national project in post-quantum cryptography and led national and international security research projects.

He has contributed expert reports to the Finnish government and given expert opinions to the Finnish Parliament. Before becoming a professor, he worked at VTT for eight years and visited the COSIC group at KU Leuven in 2014–2015. Since July 2024, he has also served on the board of Xiphera, a Finnish cryptographic hardware company.
